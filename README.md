# Hi, I’m Daniel Xu 👋

### Product leader, builder, and investor connecting technology, capital, and real-world execution.

I’m a Toronto-based Senior Product Manager at **IBM** and a hands-on software builder. My product career also includes positions with **Tencent** and **Elections Ontario**, giving me experience across global technology, public-sector digital services, and enterprise product environments.

Alongside product development, I have investment experience at **Amber Equities** and **Pinetree Capital**. Through **[New Billionaires Club](https://nbclub.ca)** and **Capital Works**, I explore the intersection of entrepreneurship, investment, community, and long-term company building.

The common thread across my work is simple: I turn complex, ambiguous problems into understandable systems and practical outcomes.
